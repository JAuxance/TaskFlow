Cookies & storage

8 October 2026

This page describes the storage used by TaskFlow. Cloudflare may set cookies needed to deliver and protect the public site. TaskFlow itself does not add an advertising or measurement cookie.

Session cookie

The session cookie keeps you signed in and protects actions against unwanted requests. It can also be created before sign-in to protect the login and registration forms. It is a browser-session cookie; browser session restoration may preserve it. The server separately limits authenticated sessions to 24 hours and revokes the current session at sign-out.

Blocking this cookie prevents normal sign-in and account actions. In production it is restricted to HTTPS and cannot be read by page JavaScript.

Your appearance preference

When you choose a light or dark theme, taskflow-theme in local storage remembers that choice on this browser. It has no automatic expiry. Clearing this site's browser data removes it and restores the system preference. It contains no account identifier.

External resources and tracking

The fonts and Socket.IO client are served with TaskFlow. The application does not include an advertising or audience-measurement script. Cloudflare may store what it needs to proxy and protect taskflow.auxance.dev.

Storage needed for authentication, security and an appearance choice does not by itself require a general “accept all cookies” banner. Any future optional tracking must be assessed before activation. CNIL guidance on cookies and trackers.
